Questions

When does it open?

With Zero's mainnet, once the contracts are deployed there. Zero's testnet comes first.

Is it audited?

Three internal reviews are done and fixed, with tests for each finding. An external audit comes before any real money.

Is there a token?

No. Submarine has no token, and nothing here promises one.

What wallet do I need?

None to start: the terminal makes one in your browser, locked with your PIN. MetaMask works too.

Where is the code?

Open to read on GitHub: the contracts, the indexer, the keeper and the site.
